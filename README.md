<h1 align="center"> PawPair 🐾 </h1>

PawPair is an end‑to‑end pet adoption and post‑adoption companion platform. Instead of simply listing animals like existing adoption sites, this app focuses on ensuring compatibility and long‑term success. The goal is to reduce failed adoptions, increase responsible pet ownership, and make the adoption journey more personalized, transparent, and fun.

## MVP 🐶
- Lifestyle quiz: Identifies the type of pet best suited for their habits, home environment, and personality
- Browse local adoptable animals: With required verified documentation, behavior insights, and previous‑owner credibility checks
- Gamified pet‑care assistant: After adoption, the app transforms into helping new owners track routines, training, health, and bonding activities
- AR or camera-based scanning: For analyzing home/pet compatibility

## Stretch Goals 😼
- Social community platform for pet parents to share insights, tips/tricks, encouraging everyday remarks
- In-App Training Modules to provide short guides or interactive lessons for basic pet trainings
- Pet Sitter Guidance allowing app to automatically transform recorded pet information and habits into a well-organized and easily followable pet care schedule or daily to-do list
